Summary Colourful interior design creates warm, welcoming homes when colour is layered thoughtfully with neutral tones, natural materials, and balanced layouts. By choosing a consistent palette and introducing colour strategically, you can design rooms that feel lively, stylish, and timeless. The best colourful interiors aren’t the brightest—they’re the ones where every colour has a purpose!
