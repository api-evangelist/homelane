# HomeLane

> HomeLane is an Indian end-to-end interior design company delivering customized home interiors — modular kitchens, wardrobes, storage, and full-room designs — pairing in-house digital design and 3D visualization tools with material selection and professional installation. Backed by Accel and headquartered in Bangalore, HomeLane operates across major Indian cities. It currently exposes no public developer API.
