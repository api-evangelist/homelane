Summary Wood brings warmth, character, and timeless elegance to modern bathrooms when used thoughtfully. By combining moisture-resistant materials, smart layouts, and carefully balanced colours, you can create a bathroom that’s both beautiful and practical for everyday living. The best wooden bathrooms don’t imitate nature—they invite it indoors through thoughtful textures, calming colours, and enduring materials.
